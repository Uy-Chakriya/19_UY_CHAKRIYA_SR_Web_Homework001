    III. Departments

    Here are some of our departmens :

    1. Cardiology

      The Cardiology department focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of heart-related diseases. Cardiologists treat conditions such as heart attacks, irregular heartbeat, and high blood pressure. The heart pumps blood at an average rate of 72 beats per minute. Oxygen is carried by blood cells using O2. Modern cardiology uses advanced equipment like ECG® machines.

    2. Pediatrics

      The Pediatrics department provides medical care for infants, children, and teenagers. Pediatricians monitor child growth and development and treat common illnesses such as fever, asthma, and infections. Children usually receive vaccines before the age of 5+. Normal body temperature is around 37°C.

    3. General Surgery

      The General Surgery department performs surgical procedures to treat various diseases and injuries. Surgeons operate on organs such as the stomach, appendix, and intestines. Patients must fast for at least 8 hours before surgery. Common surgical tools include scalpels and forceps.

    4. Internal Medicine

      The Internal Medicine department focuses on preventing, diagnosing, and treating adult diseases. Internists manage chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and kidney disease. Blood sugar levels are measured in mg/dL*. A healthy pH level is about 7.4normal.
